## What changed

`ProtectedExecutor` now consumes each unique permit atomically before dispatch, expires it, and checks its original policy/authority context against current state. Authority and policy revisions prevent restoration from reviving old permits. Outstanding capacity is bounded. A failure to record pre-dispatch evidence invalidates the associated permit. The existing evidence exception distinguishes a completed operation from failure to persist its success record.

The public six-scenario API, browser assets and scenario catalog were not changed. The branch is proposed reference work only; it does not change the live demo until an authorized merge/deployment.
