## What the benchmark is testing

The point is not to optimize a synthetic number. The point is to require a proposed agentic redesign to declare measurable outcomes **before** adoption, while preserving governance evidence for consequential actions.

A useful workflow experiment should be able to answer:

1. What was the baseline?
2. Which steps changed?
3. Which steps gained AI capability?
4. Which steps gained execution authority?
5. Where was human approval retained?
6. What did the redesign improve?
7. Which controls fired during execution?
8. Can an independent reviewer reconstruct the decision path?
